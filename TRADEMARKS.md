# Trademark and Logo Usage

This repository contains software, metadata, and tooling under the MIT License.
The logo artwork is different: each company or institution retains its own
trademarks and brand assets, including non-financial Nigerian and African brands.

Using this repository does not grant ownership of any logo or permission to use a
third-party trademark in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership, or
affiliation.

Officially verified logo assets require sources such as:

- The institution's official website.
- Official brand or media kit pages.
- Official annual reports, investor PDFs, or regulatory documents.
- Other institution-owned domains or files that clearly publish the asset.

Maintainers may remove any asset when its source cannot be verified, the brand
owner requests removal, or the logo is outdated.

V2 can also expose manually reviewed community artwork, including historical
versions. It must retain its community source and non-official status; review does
not establish trademark permission or relicense the artwork. The original catalog
is not automatically replaced by these additions.

Country flag artwork imported from `flag-icons@7.6.0` is separately MIT licensed;
the upstream license is shipped at `/flags/v1/LICENSE.txt` and in flag-containing
ZIPs. This does not license company logos or imply official national proportions,
government endorsement, or corporate/political affiliation. See [flags](docs/flags.md).
