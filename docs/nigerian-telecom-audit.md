# Nigerian Telecom Coverage Audit

Reviewed 18 October 2026. This is a brand/artwork audit, not a definitive list of currently licensed or active Nigerian operators. Historical subscriber and numbering records must not be presented as proof of present-day service.

## Why 9mobile Looked Missing

- The current operator is published as **T2 Mobile**. Its [official company history](https://t2mobile.com.ng/about-us) explicitly says it was formerly known as 9mobile.
- The existing alias `9mobile` matched that exact spelling, but the V2 search used a literal substring. `9 mobile` and `9-mobile` did not match. Search and request-form duplicate suggestions now normalize spaces, punctuation, case and diacritics consistently.
- The primary artwork is T2's current orange identity, not the former green 9mobile mark. The historical 9mobile logo is now a separate **previous version** on the same V2 brand. It has SVG, PNG and WebP downloads, sharing, code copy, reporting and bag support.
- The historical asset comes from a [community utility-logo repository](https://github.com/josephajibodu/utility-providers-assets/blob/main/network-providers/9mobile.svg). It is labelled historical and community-sourced, **not officially verified**. Its source checksum and review record are separate from T2's official primary artwork. Trademark rights remain with the owner; no redistribution licence is claimed.
