# Requested Services Coverage

Reviewed 2026-10-19. The request contains 31 brands: 29 additive V2 records and two existing records (Swift Networks and Coollink). All prior logo records remain unchanged.

## Batches

- `requested-services-mobility-retail-2026-10-19`: 19 brands across retail, connectivity, mobility, delivery, travel and financial infrastructure.
- `requested-services-media-2026-10-19`: 10 television and streaming identities.

Both batches stay below the 25-brand publication limit. The candidate seed file and manifest record the complete review trail. Current V2 coverage becomes 381 brands: 228 legacy and 153 additions.

## Identity Decisions

- Cowry is the Lagos transport-payment app, explicitly confirmed by the user, not Cowrywise.
- Renee uses the user-supplied `instagram.com/reneesupermarket` profile. The original small raster is retained without upscaling.
- GIGGO uses its developer-supplied app icon, not the parent GIG Logistics logo. StarTimes and StarTimes ON have distinct artwork.
- Wakanow uses its developer-supplied app emblem. The regional-domain wordmark was excluded because independent ownership of that source was not established.
- FoodCourt's header SVG embeds a PNG. Original PNG bytes are extracted, not traced or represented as a true SVG.
- NolliStream uses its official developer app icon. Movie posters and download badges from the homepage are excluded.
- The Smile website supplies a logo containing a Beta label. It is preserved as provided, not retouched.
- EbonyLife refers to the EbonyLife TV identity in this batch, not the newer umbrella group or every subsidiary.

## Provenance and Archival Brands

Twenty-three additions use company-controlled websites, social profiles or developer listings. DStv, GOtv, IROKOtv, EbonyLife TV and Showmax use manually reviewed community-source artwork and remain `needs-review`, never officially verified. DStv and GOtv use their actual brand wordmarks, not MyDStv/MyGOtv app icons, MultiChoice branding or programme artwork.

BoxOffice uses an original raster extracted from page 16 of the NFVF's 2021 box-office report. This regulator publication is not artwork verification by the brand owner; the record is therefore classified as third-party/community artwork, not officially verified. The hashed source PDF is retained under research evidence. No raster is traced or upscaled.

Showmax's owner announced forthcoming discontinuation in 2026 at <https://www.canalplusgroup.com/en/mandatoryoffermultichoice/multichoice-post-76>. Its asset is an archival brand identity, not a claim of active streaming availability. Likewise, historical Mobitel, BoxOffice, EbonyLife TV and IROKOtv branding does not establish current service availability. Nigerian market evidence includes historical operations where appropriate. Unknown origin and headquarters countries remain null rather than assigning foreign-founded companies a Nigerian origin.

Official source provenance is not a grant of trademark permission. Brand owners retain their rights; community artwork retains its non-official attribution.

## Compatibility

`services-baseline.json` protects the complete prior 124 expansion records. The legacy baseline protects V1 assets and metadata separately. Existing V2 preview, sharing, reporting, code-copy, pack and mixed ZIP workflows consume the same catalog format. This change does not deploy production or publish a plugin update.
